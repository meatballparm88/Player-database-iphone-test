Frequently asked questions

What is Meatballparm?

An offline fantasy-football draft research app — rankings, values, tiers, targets, injury context, and original scouting notes, all in one place.

Does it work without a connection?

Yes. The full player database is bundled inside the app, so it works completely offline. When you're online, it can quietly refresh with the latest data.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no login. Your saved targets and preferences are stored right on your device.

Where does the data come from?

Player statistics are compiled from publicly available sources; the scouting notes and analysis are our own. Meatballparm is not affiliated with the NFL or NFLPA.

Is my information private?

Yes — we don't collect personal information, track you, or show ads. See our Privacy Policy for the details.

How do I delete my data?

Everything you save lives on your device, so deleting the app removes it. There's no account to close.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by the app stores, so refunds go through them: Apple via reportaproblem.apple.com, and Google Play via the Play Store's order history. We're glad to help if something isn't working.

I found a bug or have a feature idea.

Please email us — real feedback from drafters shapes the app.
